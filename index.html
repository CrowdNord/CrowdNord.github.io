How we work

Four promises we keep. They match our post on X.

  1. We never ask for your password or 2FA codes.

    Official support will not ask for them either. Anyone who does is a scammer.

  2. We only use official recovery channels.

    We walk you through the platform’s own forms and help pages. We do not sell bypasses, hacks, or “backdoor” access.

  3. We never DM first.

    If an account claiming to be CrowdNord messages you first, it is not us. You can check by starting from this site or from @CrowdNord.

  4. We don’t promise guaranteed results.

    Recovery is decided by the platform. We will tell you honestly what we can and cannot help with.

If you want help with official recovery steps, DM @CrowdNord or reach us on any of the channels in our contact section.

What happens after you DM us

  1. You contact us first.

    Send a DM on X or an email. We do not cold-message people about locked accounts.

  2. We ask only what we need to point you to the right official flow.

    Which platform, what you can still see, and what already happened (password change, appeal filed, and so on). We will not ask for your password, 2FA codes, backup codes, or remote access to your device.

  3. We guide you through the official recovery pages.

    You submit the forms yourself, from a device you control. We help you avoid phishing links and common mistakes.

  4. We are honest about limits.

    If the case is something we cannot help with, or if a restore looks unlikely, we will say so. We cannot override a platform’s decision.

  5. Any fee is explained before work starts.

    If there is nothing to pay, we will say that too. We will never ask for gift cards, crypto, or payment in a private chat that does not match this site.

Scam warning signs

Account-recovery scams are common. If you see any of the signs below, stop, block, and report the account. Then use only the official links on this page.

  • Someone asks for your password, 2FA codes, or backup codes

    Official support will not ask for these. We will not either. Anyone who does is a scammer.

  • Someone asks for remote access to your device

    Do not install remote-access apps or share your screen with anyone offering to “recover” your account.

  • A recovery link arrives in a DM or a reply

    Do not follow it. Bookmark the official pages listed above and open them yourself.

  • An account claiming to be CrowdNord messages you first

    We never DM first. If they message you first, it is not us. Start from this site or from @CrowdNord, and check that the handle is exactly CrowdNord.

  • Someone contacts you from an account not listed on this page

    Our only accounts are listed in the Contact section: @CrowdNord on X, Instagram, and Telegram, WhatsApp +1 (513) 780-9655, and crowdnord@yahoo.com. Anyone else claiming to be CrowdNord is a scammer.

  • They ask for gift cards, crypto, or payment in a private chat

    We will never ask for gift cards or crypto. Any fee is explained before work starts, in writing, in a way that matches this site.

  • Replies that say “DM this expert” or promise a sure restore

    That is a common scam. Block and report them. Nobody can guarantee a platform will restore an account.

  • A request for ID photos or login codes in a DM

    Do not send ID photos or login codes to anyone who DMs you. Submit official forms only on the platform’s own site, from a device you control.
